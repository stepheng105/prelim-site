Frequently Asked Questions

Are the written solutions on this site public?

No. Only problem statements, metadata (concepts, theorems, difficulty, author), and the tools built around them are public. Written solutions live in a private repository and are never published.

How is difficulty determined?

Difficulty is obviously quite subjective. Something you may consider to be easy could be very difficult for someone else. However, for the sake of classification, I tried my best to assign difficulty in the following fashion:

  • 1: Extremely standard proofs or quick one-liners
  • 2: Proofs that are quick applications of a theorem or concept, or a semi-standard proof that is not too hard to remember
  • 3: Average difficulty. Probably uses a theorem or two, but applies the concepts in a standard way. Generally could be done if you encountered it for the first time. This should make up most questions
  • 4: Requires an idea that is not very obvious, is a bit long/involved, or is a long multi-step problem. Sometimes combines multiple ideas in one question, using multiple theorems/concepts
  • 5: Very long and/or requires ideas that are not clear or easy to see. Often more technically challenging, combine many different ideas, and often are proofs that would be difficult to come up with on your own without having seen it before.
